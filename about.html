A LITTLE ABOUT ME

About

Photo

HELLO!

Hiii! I'm a young artist based in the US. I've been drawing for funsies since I was a kid. Drawing is my biggest and most important hobby (so far). I mainly draw digitally (shout out to my iPad). This website is a collection of some of my work.

I have a more "anime-ish" artstyle; I rarely do realism (in case you couldn't tell). I like making art of characters that feel special to me! I am still on my way to develop my own unique art style(s) and experiment with different techniques. Right now I have two recognizable art styles.

I am still improving and got a long way to go!

~'Chyien'
